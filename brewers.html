About the Foundation

The Brewers Community Foundation (BCF) is the 501(c)(3) non-profit arm of the Milwaukee Brewers Baseball Club. Since our inception, we have contributed millions of dollars to local nonprofits that provide quality programming in the areas of health, education, recreation, and basic needs.

Our work is fueled by the incredible fans of the Milwaukee Brewers. Whether it’s through the 5K Famous Racing Sausages Run or our annual Drive for Charity, every dollar raised stays right here in Greater Milwaukee to ensure our neighbors have the resources they need to thrive.

Our History: Founded to harness the passion of baseball for social good, BCF has grown into a cornerstone of Milwaukee philanthropy.

Our Vision: A community where every child has a safe place to play and every student has the tools to succeed.
